FAQ
Common questions
How is my delivery price calculated?
Price depends on package size, weight, and whether a cross-border permit is needed. Request a quote and our team confirms the exact total before you pay — no surprise charges.
How long does delivery take between Singapore and Malaysia?
We commit to pickup and delivery within 2 days, on either side of the border. You'll get a confirmed pickup window when your quote is accepted.
What can I send?
Most parcels and documents are fine. Some items are restricted or prohibited from crossing the border — message us if you're not sure about a specific item before booking.
How do I pay?
Cashless only — pay with DuitNow or Touch 'n Go eWallet once your quote is confirmed.
Can I track my parcel?
Yes — you'll see live status updates as it's picked up, on the way, and delivered.
Do you handle customs and permit paperwork?
Yes — permit and customs paperwork for the Singapore ⇄ Malaysia crossing is handled as part of the service.
